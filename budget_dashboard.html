    How the Budget passes Parliament

      Reading the figures

      Appropriations are limits, not spending

      An appropriation is the most a department can spend for a purpose. Actual spending is usually lower. Appropriations also include capital spending and transfers, so the total is not the same as the Government's expenses in its financial statements.

      Identified transfers

      Some appropriations move assets or money between parts of the government rather than paying for services. In 2026/27 the largest is the transfer of school property assets to the New Zealand School Property Agency. Most totals on this site leave identified transfers out. Tick "Include identified transfers" to add them back.

      Multi-category appropriations

      A multi-category appropriation (MCA) is shown once as a total and again as its categories. Adding both together counts the money twice, so totals on this site use the MCA total only.

      Units and rounding

      The Estimates show amounts in $000 (thousands of dollars). Tables and charts on this site use $000 too, so they can be checked against the Vote documents. The key figures at the top of pages are rounded to $ billion or $ million so they are easy to read.

      Which year is compared

      Changes compare the 2026/27 Budget with the 2025/26 final budgeted amount (after the Supplementary Estimates). This is the comparison the Treasury uses in the Vote documents.
