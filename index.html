Parliamentary Library

Research Services for Parliament

The Parliamentary Library is a working library that provides research services for members of Parliament and parliamentary staff. We also answer requests from the public for information on any aspect of Parliament.

We have created a pilot platform to share selected tools, dashboards, maps and visualisations developed by the Research Services Team.

    About this site



    Pilot initiative

    This website is a pilot initiative of the Parliamentary Library Research Services Team, bringing together selected tools, dashboards, maps and data products.


    Tools may be added, changed or removed as we learn what is most useful to our users. Have feedback? Let us know at parlinfo@parliament.govt.nz

    Website Content

    The content on our website is provided for general information only. While we make every effort to ensure the information is accurate, it may contain errors or omissions. It should not be treated as advice or as an official position of the Library or of Parliament.

    Data and sources

    Data is sourced from publically avaliable information only. Sources are noted on each tool.
